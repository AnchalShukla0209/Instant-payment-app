# Instant Payment Website — Content & Information Architecture

Status: Step 1 complete (content inventory and structure)

## 1. Positioning

**Brand:** Instant Payment / IP Instant Payment Technology Private Limited

**Primary positioning:** A technology-led assisted financial services platform that helps Indian retailers and entrepreneurs deliver everyday banking, payment and digital services.

**Primary audience:**

- Retailers and local shop owners
- Distributors and master distributors
- Rural and semi-urban entrepreneurs
- Customers looking for assisted digital services
- Prospective business and technology partners

**Core message:** Payments that move India forward.

**Supporting message:** One trusted platform for banking, payments and digital services—built to help every retailer serve, earn and grow.

## 2. Primary conversion goals

1. Become a retailer/partner
2. Become a distributor
3. Log in to the partner platform

## 4. Homepage content architecture

### 01 — Fixed header

- Dark-surface logo on white glass navigation
- Product, Partner Network, Company and Resource navigation
- Primary action: Partner Login
- Secondary action: Become a Partner

### 02 — Hero

**Eyebrow:** Powering Financial Inclusion

**Headline:** Payments that move India forward.

**Body:** One trusted platform for banking, payments and digital services—built to help every retailer serve, earn and grow.

**Actions:** Become a Partner / Explore Services

**Visual:** AI-generated financial technology ecosystem, concentrated on the right with clear copy space on the left.

### 03 — Product ecosystem

Present the platform as a connected ecosystem instead of a basic service list:

- Assisted Banking
- Payments & Collections
- Utility Services
- Retail Partner Network
- Business Technology

Only categories backed by live services should be published.

### 04 — Trust and compliance strip

Show approved certifications, banking/technology partners and compliance badges.

**Publishing rule:** No PCI DSS, ISO, RBI, NPCI, IRDAI or partner-bank badge should be shown until documentary approval is provided.

### 05 — About and impact

**Headline direction:** Turning local shops into digital service centres.

Explain financial inclusion, assisted services and retailer income opportunity.

Potential proof points (approval required):

- Retail/partner count
- States or districts covered
- Successful customers/transactions
- Years in operation
- Number of live services

### 06 — Brand ambassador / leadership story

Use one consistent person across About, Partner Network and Why Choose Us.

Recommended option: a fictional AI-generated Indian fintech ambassador, age 30–40, formal navy suit, approachable expression, consistent identity across all images.

Alternative: use an authorised founder/team member. Written image approval and 4–6 high-quality reference photographs will be required.

Do not use a celebrity or public figure without explicit licensing rights.

### 07 — Services suite

Verified from the application code:

- AEPS
- Domestic Money Transfer
- Bill Payments / BBPS-type categories
- Mobile Recharge
- Credit Card Bill Payment
- FASTag Bill Payment
- Settlement workflows

Candidate services requiring business confirmation:

- Micro ATM
- Insurance distribution
- PAN services
- Loans
- Account opening
- Travel or ticketing

### 08 — Security

Safe copy can discuss security principles without making unverified certification claims:

- Strong authentication
- Encrypted communication
- Role-based access
- Transaction monitoring
- Secure partner onboarding

Specific audit and certification claims require evidence before publishing.

### 09 — Partner opportunity

Two conversion paths:

1. Retailer onboarding
2. Distributor onboarding

Suggested flow: Apply → Complete KYC → Get activated → Start serving.

### 10 — Technology platform

Present the operational strengths visible in the application:

- Multiple user roles
- Provider routing
- Transaction reporting
- Service availability controls
- Settlement workflows
- Notifications and support

Do not advertise public APIs unless an API product and documentation actually exist.

### 11 — Why choose Instant Payment

- Essential digital services in one platform
- Secure transaction workflows
- Retailer and distributor network model
- Operational reporting
- Partner assistance
- Designed for emerging India

### 12 — Testimonials / partner stories

Use real, approved partner stories wherever possible. Each testimonial should have consent, name, city/state, role and photograph approval.

Until approvals are available, testimonials must be labelled as placeholders and must not be published as real customer endorsements.

### 13 — Media and updates

Display only verifiable press links, company announcements or awards. Hide this section if no approved items are available.

### 14 — FAQ and conversion CTA

FAQ topics:

- What is Instant Payment?
- Who can become a partner?
- Which documents are required?
- Which services can a retailer offer?
- How does onboarding work?
- How can a distributor join?
- Where can an existing partner log in?

Final CTA: Ready to bring digital services to your community?

### 15 — Legal footer

- Registered company name
- Registered and operating address
- CIN and applicable registrations
- Support, grievance and nodal contacts
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Grievance/Escalation Policy
- Security warning: never share OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords
- Social links
